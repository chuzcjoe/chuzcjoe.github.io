Kids event hosted by Centerville Library.

<!-- more -->

Many libraries located at East Bay will host kids-friendly events/activities every weekend. This weekend at Centerville Library, it was petting zoo day. More events can be found at this [website](https://aclibrary.bibliocommons.com/v2/events?_gl=1*1iz8ueb*_ga*NzIxNTg4MzkyLjE3NTMwNzY2Mjk.*_ga_G99DMMNG39*czE3NTMwNzY2MjgkbzEkZzAkdDE3NTMwNzY2NTAkajM4JGwwJGgw*_ga_DJ3QFJ52TT*czE3NTMwNzY2MjgkbzEkZzAkdDE3NTMwNzY2MjgkajYwJGwwJGgw&locations=FRM).
